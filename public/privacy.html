Privacy Policy

Last Updated: 9 November 2026

This policy explains in plain English how AvenZo handles your information.

1. Who We Are 2. Information We Collect 3. How We Use Your Information 4. Who We Share It With 5. Cookies and Tracking 6. Advertising and Affiliate Links 7. How Long We Keep It 8. Your Choices and Rights 9. Security 10. Children 11. Changes to This Policy

01Who We Are

AvenZo, operated by OPG Studios, is a cashback and shopping rewards platform in India. This policy explains what personal information we handle and why. Contact: contact@avenzoopg.in. Support: https://support.avenzoopg.in/.

02Information We Collect

  • Account details you give us, such as your name, email address and phone number.
  • Payout details, such as the UPI ID you enter to withdraw cashback.
  • Purchase and tracking information that partner stores or tracking providers share with us, such as order status and order value, used to confirm cashback.
  • Technical information, such as device type, browser, IP address and pages viewed.
  • Messages and proof you send us, for example in an appeal.

03How We Use Your Information

  • To run your account and show your cashback status.
  • To track, verify and pay cashback and withdrawals.
  • To prevent fraud and misuse.
  • To answer support requests and appeals.
  • To improve and secure AvenZo, and to meet legal duties.

04Who We Share It With

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only as needed with partner stores and tracking providers (to confirm purchases), payment and payout providers such as AvenPay and UPI services (to send withdrawals), service providers who help us run AvenZo, and authorities when the law requires it.

05Cookies and Tracking

AvenZo uses cookies and similar technologies to keep you signed in, to credit purchases made through our links, and to understand how the site is used. You can control cookies in your browser, but cashback tracking may not work if they are blocked.

06Advertising and Affiliate Links

AvenZo may show ads and affiliate links and may earn a commission from partner stores. Those stores have their own privacy practices once you visit them.

07How Long We Keep It

We keep information only as long as needed for the purposes above, for example to handle disputes, prevent fraud and meet legal and accounting duties. After that we delete or anonymise it.

08Your Choices and Rights

Under applicable Indian law you may ask us to access, correct or delete your personal information, or to withdraw your consent. Email contact@avenzoopg.in and we will respond within a reasonable time. Deleting your information may mean we can no longer pay unconfirmed cashback.

09Security

We use reasonable safeguards to protect your information. No online service is completely secure, so please keep your login details private and enter your UPI ID carefully.

10Children

AvenZo is for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

11Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last Updated" date above shows the latest version. Your use of AvenZo is also covered by our Terms.
